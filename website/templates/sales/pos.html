DBMS Transactional Integrity & ACID Guarantees {{ db_engine }}

Every sale runs inside one transaction: stock is re-checked (and row-locked on MySQL InnoDB), the bill and its items are inserted, and stock is decremented. Any failure rolls the whole sale back. Prices always come from the database.
